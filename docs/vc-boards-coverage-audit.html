Priority sources to add

Ordered by yield per unit of effort. Tier 1 is config only. Tier 2 is a scraper module each. Tier 3 is the one new mechanism worth building, and it is where most of the remaining coverage sits.

Tier 1 · this week

Sources rows on the existing endpoints, zero code

Each is one row in the Sources table (Name, URL, Worker endpoint, Platform, Active). Nine come straight from the investor tally; the rest were the strongest London-relevant boards from this morning's board survey. Roughly +16 employers, taking funded-startup coverage from 43% to about 58%.

  • General Catalyst/getro?host=jobs.generalcatalyst.com
    19k listings; volume board, the term loop and 7-day window do the narrowing. Perk, Goodstack, Integral.
  • Partech/getro?host=portfoliojobs.partechpartners.com
    Maze, Shippeo, Blink. Paris-led but strong London and remote-EU portfolio.
  • Lightspeed/consider?host=jobs.lsvp.com
    15k listings, 513 companies. Granola, tem. Consider returns 100 per term so volume is not a problem.
  • Creandum/consider?host=careers.creandum.com
    Cerrion, Monta. Nordic-heavy but two live GTME-shaped roles in the gap set.
  • Cherry Ventures/getro?host=talent.cherry.vc
    Cortea, Integral. Founding GTM Engineer, Head of GTM and RevOps Manager live on the board today.
  • Moonfire/getro?host=positions.moonfire.com
    London AI-native seed. Humaans (GTM Engineer live), Orchestra, Flagright, Mindstone.
  • HV Capital/getro?host=jobs.hvcapital.com
    Fleek, Storyblok. DACH-led with London portfolio.
  • Headline/getro?host=jobs.headline.com
    Monta, Heidi Health.
  • Crane Venture Partners/getro?host=careers.crane.vc
    London B2B seed. Encord, SenseOn. Board links out to the company ATS, so links are canonical.
  • Point72 Ventures/consider?host=consider.com (board id point72-ventures)
    Heidi Health, Nscale. Hosted on consider.com rather than a vanity domain, so the host allowlist needs the board-id form; the new Board ID column on Sources covers it.
  • Point Nine, Firstminute, Backed, Outlier, Speedinvest/getro?host= jobs.pointnine.com · jobs.firstminute.capital · talent.backed.vc · jobs.outlierventures.io · careers.speedinvest.com
    Quality adds from the board survey, not the gap tally: incident.io GTM Engineer (Point Nine), n8n and Wayve (Firstminute), Thought Machine and CloudNC (Backed), Zinc GTM Operations Lead (Outlier). Low volume, London-heavy.
  • Playfair, GTMfund/consider?host= careers.playfair.vc · jobs.gtmfund.com
    Playfair is London pre-seed; GTMfund is the GTM-operator fund whose portfolio is sales-tech by construction. Both Consider.
Tier 2 · spikes

One scraper module each, worth a half-session

  • Y Combinator · Work at a Startupworkatastartup.com/jobs
    The single biggest fund in the gap (Humaans, Solve, Encord, Cerrion, Fleek, Saturn). Own platform; returns 406 to a plain fetch, so look for the JSON search endpoint the page calls before resorting to a browser. Filter to UK / remote-Europe or it floods.
  • a16zjobs.a16z.com
    ElevenLabs, Pit, Fleek. Custom Next.js aggregator, 19k jobs, US-heavy; already a deferred spike in the plan. Look for __NEXT_DATA__ or the internal API. Lowest priority of the three.
  • Techstars/getro?host=jobs.techstars.com
    Technically a Sources row (Getro, 4.8k listings) but global and founder-role heavy, so treat it as an experiment: add, watch a week, keep only if the London yield holds. Cledara GTM Engineer was live on it.
Tier 3 · build

Per-company ATS poller over the Employers table

The largest remaining block, roughly 43 of the 103 fundable employers, and the only route to three groups the boards structurally cannot reach. Same Worker, four new endpoints, driven by rows in the Employers table the way Sources drives the boards, feeding the same email contract so job-sweep needs nothing new.

  • Endpoints/ashby?slug= · /greenhouse?slug= · /lever?slug= · /workable?slug= · /teamtailor?host=
    All public JSON or RSS, no scraping: api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{slug}, boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{slug}/jobs (EU boards on job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io), api.lever.co/v0/postings/{slug}, apply.workable.com/api/v1/widget/accounts/{slug}, {careers-site}/jobs.rss. Ashby was the most common ATS in the sweep; Teamtailor second.
  • Who goes on the list first3 groups, ~50 companies
    Growth and PE-backed scale-ups with no investor board (Nscale, ClearScore, Attest, CUBE, Unity Advisory, Hostaway, Prevalent, Trackunit). Portfolio companies of scraped funds whose boards do not carry them (Causaly, Apron, Birdie, Uncapped, Vitesse, SenseOn, Maze). Vendor services teams that hire GTME under other titles (Clay, Apollo, HubSpot, Cognism, n8n: Solutions Engineer, Growth Strategist, Forward Deployed).
  • Then the no-board seed funds' portfolios20VC · Visionaries Club · Next47 · Frontline · Eight Roads · Episode 1 · 10x Founders
    These are the specialist London and European seed investors you would most expect to run boards, and none do. Their portfolio pages list the companies; each company becomes an Employers row with its ATS slug.
Tier 4 · backstops

Cheap weekly tripwires, not daily feeds

  • Clay community share-jobs RSS (community.clay.com/x/share-jobs/rss.xml): the one machine-readable source for agency-side GTME work; mostly contractor and US-timezone, but it is where Punch! posted a Lead GTM Engineer with your exact stack.
  • Cargo GTM board (getcargo.ai/jobs): plain HTML, 974 roles, London filter; holds 0-3 UK roles at a time (HowNow, Ably, LILT this week).
  • Adzuna API (free key, UK keyword + location): the cheap net for companies not yet on any list, on "GTM Engineer", "Go-to-Market Engineer", "GTM Systems", "Revenue Systems".
  • Glassdoor and Indeed email alerts on the same titles, into the existing sweep as senders; Glassdoor had 7 literal GTME hits of its first 15 for London.
Not worth it

Looked at, leave out

  • HubSpot Elite partners (Huble, Six & Flow, BabelQuest, Digital Litmus): real hiring, custom careers pages, roughly £45k roles.
  • US-only volume boards with little UK (Khosla, Redpoint, Stage 2 Capital unless remote-first roles are in scope).
  • First Round (login-gated), Founders Factory (board renders empty), the GTME micro-boards (gtm-engineer-jobs.com, GTME HQ, nRev: zero UK).
  • Members-only communities (Pavilion, RevOps Co-op, WizOps, Modern Sales Pros): US remote and behind login; a proactive-outreach channel, not a feed.

How this was measured

Population: every Roles Inventory row with a GTM-family title (gtm, go-to-market, revops, revenue or sales or marketing operations, martech, growth or marketing or lifecycle or solutions engineer, forward deployed, GTM systems) and a UK, remote, EMEA or blank location, first seen since 4 June 2026, that arrived via a non-board channel. 306 rows, 254 employers. Board side: the Worker called once per board with days=90&loc=all, 508 distinct companies with GTM-family listings. Employer names normalised (legal suffixes, taglines, punctuation) before matching.

Caveats that push the true figure up a little: Getro returns 20 cards per search term, newest first, so older listings on busy boards are truncated in a 90-day pull; two alias misses (Ably Realtime, Owner.com) counted as gaps. Investor data is one press search per company; stage and lead investor are as reported, not verified against Companies House. The 43 / 58 / 90 percent ladder assumes a board or ATS feed carries the role when the company is on it, which the opt-in point above shows is optimistic.

Data: Roles Inventory (Airtable), VC Job Sweeper Worker, one-pass web research on 2 Sep 2026. Full workings in the project doc gtme-sourcing-research.md.
