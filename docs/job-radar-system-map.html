The nightly run, step by step

What happens each weekday at 01:00 London. It takes 6 to 8 minutes, mostly writing to Airtable one row at a time.

  1. Read the config.

    Sources rows that are Active; Startup Universe rows with Poll ticked and an ATS slug; Keyword Sources rows that are Active, not email alerts, with a Worker endpoint.

  2. Call the Worker.

    All boards at once. Company feeds 8 at a time every 1.5 seconds, so Workable doesn't rate-limit. Keyword sources 4 at a time, one call per line in the row's Keywords.

  3. Write status back.

    Last run, last error and listings pulled on each Sources, Startup Universe and Keyword Sources row. A failure here never stops the run.

  4. Merge and dedupe on link.

    A role found on a board and on the company's own feed is one role.

  5. Check the guard.

    Only the boards count: under 20 listings or more than 5 failed boards sends "VC Boards Sweep - FAILED" and stops. Dead ATS slugs and keyword errors are listed in the email instead.

  6. Upsert Raw Listings on link.

    Last seen is set to today; First seen is stamped on new rows.

  7. Email what's new.

    Rows seen today and never emailed, with source, location, posted date and salary where stated. Sent even on zero-new days.

  8. Stamp Emailed on, then tidy.

    Emailed rows are stamped so a re-run can't send them twice. Rows first seen more than 30 days ago are deleted.

Where data lives

The split: pipes and run logs in one base, companies and roles you act on in the other. Airtable can't link records across bases, which is why Startup Universe sits with Employers.

VC Job Sweeper

Plumbing. What to fetch, and what was fetched.

appv8Lxbh4kp6DoBv
SourcesOne row per VC board. Nightly run reads it and writes last scraped, last error and count back.
Keyword SourcesOne row per whole-market source, including email alerts for reference. Nightly run reads the active non-email rows.
Raw ListingsEvery role the run has seen, keyed on link. Source of the nightly email. Rows older than 30 days are deleted.
TasksThe live backlog for this system: status, owner, priority and effort per task.
